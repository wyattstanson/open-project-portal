# Open Project Portal — Student Guide

A short walkthrough of how to sign in, build your project team, and get it approved.
The whole process happens on the portal — you never email spreadsheets around.

> **The rule for every team:** exactly **5 members = 2 from SCOPE + 3 from three
> different other schools**. The portal checks this for you as you build your team.

---

## 1. Signing in for the first time

Your login is your **registration number** (e.g. `24BCE2312`).

1. Collect your one-time **hashkey** from your **proctor**. It is a 16–26 character
   code that is unique to you.
2. On the portal, choose **Student → Continue as student**.
3. Enter your registration number, and type the **hashkey** in the password box.
4. You will be asked to **create your own password**. It must have:
   - at least **8 characters**
   - a **lowercase** letter, an **uppercase** letter, a **number**, and an **underscore** ( `_` )
   - it must be **different from every other student's** password
5. That's it — from now on you sign in with your registration number and the
   password you just created. The hashkey is used only once.

**Forgot your password?** Use the *Forgot password?* link, or ask the admin. They
will reset you, and you'll register again with your hashkey.

---

## 2. Building your team

Only a **SCOPE** student can create and lead a team. If you are in another school,
a SCOPE team leader invites you — the invitation appears under **My group**.

**If you are a SCOPE leader:**

1. Go to **Find teammates**. Search by name or registration number.
2. Press **Invite** next to a student. They receive a pending invitation.
   - A person can only hold **one invitation at a time** — once you invite someone,
     they disappear from everyone else's list until they accept or decline.
3. Watch the **Team constraints** checklist fill in. It shows every rule and
   highlights in **red** whatever is still missing (e.g. "Exactly 3 members from
   other schools — 2 / 3").
4. You can **Remove** anyone you invited if plans change; that frees them again.

**If you were invited:** open **My group**, and press **Accept** or **Decline**.
Declining frees you to join a different team.

Your team is ready when all five members have accepted and every constraint is green.

---

## 3. Choosing a faculty guide

1. Once your team is complete, the leader opens **My group** and picks a faculty
   from the **Select faculty** list (it shows how many slots each guide has left).
2. Press **Choose faculty**. You'll see **"Request sent to …"**.
3. The request goes **only** to that faculty. They review your team and **accept**
   or **reject** it. Each faculty accepts a limited number of teams.

> Requests that aren't accepted by **midnight (IST)** are released automatically,
> so no slot stays locked overnight — just choose a faculty again the next day.

You can track your status any time under **My group**: *awaiting members*,
*request sent*, or *approved*.

---

## 4. Getting help

- Use **Get help** in the portal to send a query straight to the admin, or use the
  **email the helpdesk** link there.
- Your email address is always **private** — other students never see it. Only
  faculty and the admin can see contact details.
