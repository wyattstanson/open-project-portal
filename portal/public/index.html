Institute Open Projects

Form your project team, get it approved.

Students build a group of five under the school rules and track its approval. Faculty review the groups that qualify and accept up to three. A student project tool, not an official institute site.

Student

Sign in with your institute email and password, submit your group of five, and see its status. Your email stays private from other students.

Faculty

Sign in with your staff credentials to review qualifying groups with real contact emails, browse the student directory, and accept up to three teams.

Administrator

Full oversight: every student and faculty account, their emails and initial passwords, remaining faculty slots, and the ability to reset a student login.
