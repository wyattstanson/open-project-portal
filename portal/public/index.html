Faculty

Administrator

Full oversight of every student and faculty account, faculty slots, support queries, password resets, and a CSV export of all allocations.

First time here? Students sign in with the one-time hashkey issued by their proctor, then set a personal password. Faculty and administrators use the credentials provided to them. Student email addresses are encrypted at rest and never shown to other students.

Upload students

Excel (.xlsx) or .csv. Replaces the student roster and updates the whole portal live.

Columns read: reg, name, email. School & SCOPE/other are derived from the reg number. Students already registered keep their password.

Upload faculty

Excel (.xlsx) or .csv. Replaces the faculty list and updates live.

Columns read: email, name, school, cabin, max_groups. New faculty get an initial password shown on the Faculty tab; existing ones keep theirs.

Tip: the cabin number entered here is what students see when they message their guide.
